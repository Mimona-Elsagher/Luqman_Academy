Student Policies & Guidelines

- Advance Payment:

All subscription fees and packages must be paid in advance prior to scheduling or starting the classes to ensure continuity.

- Cancellation Notice Period:

Any cancellation or request to reschedule a class must be made at least 1 hour before the session starts.

- Late Cancellation & No-Show:

If a cancellation is made less than 1 hour before the session, or in case of a student's no-show, the class will be fully counted (deducted from the package)

- Student Apology & Makeup Classes:

If a student apologizes for missing a class with prior notice (more than 1 hour), alternative makeup classes can be arranged if the student requests them

- Teacher Absence & Replacement:

In the rare event that a teacher is absent, the academy guarantees a free makeup class for the student or a qualified substitute teacher to ensure no disruption in the learning plan.

- Student Lateness & Teacher Availability:

If a student is late for a session, the teacher will remain in the virtual classroom throughout the entire duration of the class. This ensures that whenever the student joins—at any time during the session—they will find their teacher waiting and ready to begin.

- Communication & Schedule Changes:

All scheduling changes, requests, or inquiries must go exclusively through the academy’s management/coordinators, not directly through the teacher.

Our Commitments to Students

- Session Reminders:

Proactive reminder notifications sent well in advance before the session, followed by a final reminder 5 minutes prior to ensure smooth class entry and attendance tracking.

- Progress Reports:

Providing comprehensive daily reports immediately after each session, alongside monthly analytical reports to closely monitor and track student development.

- Achievement Certificates:

Issuing appreciation and achievement certificates to students upon achieving progress

- 24/7 Support Team:

Providing round-the-clock technical and administrative support to handle schedule coordination, inquiries, requests, and feedback seamlessly.

- Expert Tutors & Specialized Curricula:

Supplying structured curricula and highly qualified, experienced teachers specialized in dealing with various age groups using the latest interactive educational tools.

- Academic Supervision:

Continuous monitoring and oversight of all classes by experienced senior academic supervisors to ensure the highest standard of education.

- Flexible Makeup Arrangements:

Arranging alternative schedules for classes missed by either the student or the teacher, whenever requested by the student.
